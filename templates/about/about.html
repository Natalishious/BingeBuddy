About Us

BingeBuddy is a movie recommendation web app created by a group of four students passionate about movies, machine learning, and web development.

We wanted to build something that could help people discover new movies based on their favorite genres, plots, and ratings — instead of endlessly scrolling through streaming services wondering what to watch next.

Our recommendation system combines movie data from MovieLens and TMDb to suggest films with similar genres and story themes. In other words: less time searching, more time binge-watching.

This project was built as part of our studies, but also as a way for us to explore machine learning, Flask, APIs, datasets, and frontend design in a real-world project.

We're still improving BingeBuddy, so if the recommendations get a little strange sometimes… we prefer to call it “experimental machine learning.”

Whether you're looking for your next favorite movie or just trying to avoid scrolling through streaming services for two hours, BingeBuddy is here to help.
